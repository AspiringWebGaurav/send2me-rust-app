# Send2Me - Features & Capabilities

Send2Me is designed to provide a premium, seamless experience for managing your files across multiple devices without relying on third-party cloud services. 

## 1. Direct Peer-to-Peer Transfers
- **No Cloud Servers:** Your files travel directly from one device to another. Send2Me acts purely as a secure bridge.
- **Lightning Fast:** Because transfers are direct, they operate at the maximum speed your network allows.
- **End-to-End Encrypted:** Every byte of data is encrypted before it leaves your device and decrypted only upon arrival.

## 2. Strict Folder Synchronization
- **Real-Time Mirroring:** Any changes made to a bonded folder on one device (creating, editing, renaming) are instantly reflected on the connected device.
- **Absolute 1-to-1 Parity:** Send2Me maintains a strict mirror. If a file is deleted on one device, it is permanently deleted on the other. 
- **Zero Waste:** There are no hidden trash folders or recycle bin dependencies. Deleted files are removed completely, maximizing your available storage space.
- **Self-Healing:** If a connection drops midway through a sync, Send2Me will automatically resume and heal the transfer queue the next time the devices connect.

## 3. Privacy by Default
- **Secure Pairing:** Devices are bonded using a secure, temporary 4-digit code.
- **Explicit Consent:** Even after bonding, major features like Folder Synchronization require explicit user consent and acceptance of terms to ensure absolute control over your files.
- **No Telemetry:** We do not track what you send, who you send it to, or how large your files are.

## 4. Premium User Experience
- **Sleek Interface:** A modern, dynamic user interface that provides clear visibility into your active connections, live transfers, and syncing activity.
- **Detailed History:** An integrated activity log tracks all successful and failed transfers, allowing you to easily monitor what files have moved across your devices.
